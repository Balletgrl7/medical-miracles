STRICT Sanitation & Safety Policy

All donations must be 100% BRAND NEW in ORIGINAL FACTORY PACKAGING. We CANNOT accept food, candy, used items, high-risk objects (choking/allergy hazards), scary themes, weapons, or Orbeez/water beads. Help us keep pediatric patients safe.

Giving Back to Community

Members gain volunteer hours and leadership opportunities by participating in drives and awareness campaigns—no matter what career path you plan to pursue!

All bake sale proceeds directly benefit Phoenix Children's Hospital (PCH), funding patient comfort kits and playroom essentials.

Officer roles involve planning large drives and leading bake sales, making a tangible difference for PCH patients.

Officers & Leadership

Our Officers are dedicated high school leaders who plan the logistical aspects of our drives and direct our bake sales. This level of involvement is a powerful way to build leadership experience while serving Phoenix Children's Hospital.

Bake Sales & Event Directing are hosted by Officers only.
